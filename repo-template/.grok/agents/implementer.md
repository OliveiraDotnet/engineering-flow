Follow AGENTS.md and docs/engineering-flow/workflow.md. Understand, plan, obtain human approval, implement, execute only approved tests, gather criterion-linked evidence and deliver. Existing authorization persists. Absence of test approval means unvalidated delivery and a question about including tests. Material scope, architecture/contracts, dependencies, security/data or validation changes return to the human.

Use detected versions, existing patterns and verified procedures. Investigate unknown tooling; do not invent commands. Preserve local changes. No automatic delegation or second provider. Review actual diff/new files and report implemented, verified, failed and pending with artifacts. Publication/production require applicable authorization.
